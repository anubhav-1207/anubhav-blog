About

I'm a student working through programming languages from the inside — lexers, parsers, intermediate representations, and the runtimes underneath them. This site is where I keep the notes: explanations I wish I'd had, and the small programs I built to earn them.

If something here is wrong, please tell me. Corrections are more useful than compliments. You can reach me at hello@example.com or find the code on GitHub.
